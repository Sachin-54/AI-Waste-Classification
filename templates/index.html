FAQs

SortSense identifies and categorizes waste into primary categories, including: Organic / Biodegradable: Food scraps, fruit peels, leaves, and compostable matter. Recyclable Material: Plastics (PET bottles, containers), paper, cardboard, glass bottles, and metal cans. Non-Recyclable / Hazardous: Household trash, styrofoam, batteries, and electronic waste (e-waste).

The AI model typically achieves 85% to 95%+ accuracy when images are captured in good lighting with a clear, single item or well-defined target object. Accuracy may vary slightly depending on image background clutter, shadows, or item overlapping.
